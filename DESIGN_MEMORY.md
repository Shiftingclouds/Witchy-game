# Design Memory

## Brand tone

- **Adjectives:** warm, comforting, mysterious, magical, never overwhelming.
- **The owner loves:** the illuminated grimoire for the screens outside the story; rose (wine) tinted buttons with a gold glow; arched "rounded window" frames with thin gold outlines; vines everywhere (gold on dark, green on parchment); glow and gold sparkle; pages that feel full, like a real used book.
- **Avoid:** screens that read as a website or dashboard (sidebar plus top bar plus cards); flat dark boxes with thin lines; empty half-pages; parchment that glares at night.

## Layout and spacing

- **Shell:** an open book on a lamplit desk, with two-page spreads and ribbon bookmarks on the right edge.
- **Density:** comfortable; pages carry marginalia rather than white space.
- **Corners:** hairline 1–3px; portraits and plates use arched tops.
- **Shadows:** offset and soft (stacked page edges, a deep drop shadow, a golden halo); no hard offset shadows.

## Typography

- **Headings:** Cormorant Garamond 500 (34px page titles); Cormorant SC for small labels.
- **Body:** EB Garamond 17–18px; italic for journal entries and margin notes.
- **Emphasis:** red-ink rubrics (#8a2b1e) for dates and labels, and gilded drop capitals in a vine-wrapped box.

## Colour

- **Desk:** umber #140b07 to #3a2214, with lamplight gold #e8bf6e and #f6d48e.
- **Parchment:** #e4cfa3 (edges #a07a45), ink #2b1a10, soft ink #553c28, gilt #b07d2a, verdigris leaves #4f6b45.
- **Buttons:** wine #6b2a38 with a radial gold light from above and a gilt outline.

## Interaction patterns

- Ribbons switch spreads, and the page fades in. Actions are rose buttons. People open their conversation.
- The scene player stays full-screen and is separate from the book.

## Accessibility rules

- **Focus:** red ink on parchment, gold on the desk.
- **Motion:** the candle, motes and gilt shimmer all stop under reduced motion.
- **Text:** follows the reading text-size setting.

## Repo conventions

- Plain CSS with tokens; lucide-react icons; ornaments as authored SVG components.
- The reference mock-up is in `docs/design/grimoire-reference/`, and the build plan in `DESIGN_PLAN.md`.
