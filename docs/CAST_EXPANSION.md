# A populated academy: cast and role coverage

Status: **proposed characters and assignments**, not additions already playable or illustrated. The user's brief explicitly permits new characters to fill academy, dormitory, sports, village, friendship, and romance roles. The supplied 45 identities are a starting cast, not a cast limit. New designs should belong beside the existing paintings.

The first expansion proposes **15 new named characters**, taking the working roster to **60**. This is a useful first group, not a final maximum. Add further classmates, upper-year students, visitors, rivals, relatives, and seasonal residents when a scene or institutional role needs them. A new character may have a small consequential part; they do not all need a romance route or a personal quest chain.

For each new person, start with an identity reference and plan neutral, warm, amused, grave, and tense expressions: **75 baseline portrait images for 15 people**. Additional shy, hurt, frightened, or action poses follow actual beats. Match the supplied portrait canvas, transparency, eye-line, texture, and wardrobe language. New identities get their own designs, not recoloured faces from the existing cast.

The earlier 132-image art estimate covered the original cast and locations. These portraits are additional. Four house dorm interiors may need three more variants beyond the one already proposed; three shop interiors are conditional on scenes entering those shops. Update download and caching budgets as assets are produced.

A new character is integrated only when their identity, role, relationships, reachable scenes, schedules, portraits, expression fallbacks, and save-safe ID exist. Keep outlines separate from the playable People page until those requirements are met. Background students can make classes and events feel busy, but must never substitute for the named recurring people who make the academy a place to live.
