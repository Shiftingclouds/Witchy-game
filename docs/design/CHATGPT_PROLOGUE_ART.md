# Lanternmere — art for the new beginning

Hello! Three paintings for the new first scenes of the game (the letter arriving, and the journey on the green train), plus the four house crests. Please read this whole file first.

## Attached with this file

- `platform-nought.png`, `home-kitchen.png` and `lantern-hall.png`: **style references.** The new paintings must look like they belong with these: the same painterly, softly detailed fantasy style, warm light with a clear source, rich but not busy.

## Rules for every image

1. **Wide 16:9 painting, at least 1672×941** (the same size and framing as the references).
2. **No people visible**, except where stated. No text, no logos, no frame, no collage.
3. Warm, cosy, a little mysterious. Light always has a source (window, lamp, sunset).
4. Save each with **exactly** the filename given. The game finds images by name.

## The paintings

### 1. `train-carriage.png`: inside the green train

The interior of a compartment on an old green steam train, late afternoon: warm wooden panelling, brass luggage racks with a few travel bags, two facing benches in worn deep-green velvet, a small table under the window. Through the window, rolling hills giving way to dark pine woods, with a glimpse of a long lake between the trees, all in golden late-afternoon light. A small unlit brass lantern sits on the seat. Cosy, slightly old-fashioned, quietly magical.

### 2. `cg-acceptance-letter.png`: the letter (close-up)

A close-up on a kitchen table in soft grey morning light: a thick cream-coloured letter, unfolded, with elegant handwritten-style lines (unreadable at this size; no real words), and beside it its envelope with a broken gold wax seal pressed with a small lantern emblem. A mug of tea steaming nearby, the edge of a window with rain-speckled glass. No people.

### 3. `journey-countryside.png`: from the train window

The view from a moving train at golden hour: a long valley of green hills and hedgerows giving way to tall dark pines, a long silver lake catching the sunset between the trees, and very far away, on a hill by the water, the tiny warm lights of a castle-like academy. A soft blur of motion at the edges. No people.

### 4–7. The four house crests

Four heraldic badges, one per house, shown when the player chooses their house and in their common room. **Square 1024×1024, transparent background**, each a shield-shaped badge in warm gold linework and soft painted colour, with the house's bird at its centre. Elegant and simple enough to read when small (about 48 pixels). No text.

- `crest-owlcombe.png`: an owl, violet-grey and gold (Curiosity).
- `crest-rookhallow.png`: a raven, deep red and gold (Courage).
- `crest-heronmere.png`: a heron, teal and gold (Kindness).
- `crest-larkspire.png`: a lark, yellow-gold (Wonder).
