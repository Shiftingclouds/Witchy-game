--- SECTION before-practical | place: classroom | time: morning | date: 2026-09-04 ---
[N] On Friday morning, you reach the charms classroom without unfolding your map.
[N] A line of damp footprints leads through the doorway. Inside, the windows are misted along their lower edges, and somebody has written their name backwards in the condensation.
[N] Each desk holds a stone mat, a dish of sand, and a square of white paper.
[N] Your lantern bumps softly against your knee as you find a place. Grey's report is tucked into your notebook: fitting sound, ordinary relighting unsuccessful, referred to the warding teachers. The red ribbon still hangs above your supper seat.

[END IF]
[IF arrival:house = rookhallow]

[Cas | neutral] I checked this one in person.
[Rowan | amused] He knocked on the door before breakfast. I've never seen a teacher answer a question while still eating toast.
[Cas | neutral] He pointed. It was quite clear.
[N] Cas takes a desk by the window. Rowan pulls out the chair beside yours.
[END IF]
[IF arrival:house = heronmere]

[IF arrival:house = larkspire]
[N] Toby walks in beside you, with Saoirse carrying the practice lamp he has borrowed from the common room.
[Toby | neutral] It was already bent.
[Saoirse | amused] So was the shelf. Together they looked level. A valuable lesson in why I shouldn't judge things from across the room.
[N] She gives the lamp to Elian to inspect before leaving for her own class. Toby watches until the teacher puts it among the usable ones.
[END IF]
[N] Rowan moves his bag from the neighbouring chair. His books remain on the floor, safely contained by a strap.
[Rowan | warm] I thought we'd have a go together, if you'd like. I've been practising the gesture. That's nearly the same as practising the spell.
[N] At the front, the teacher unpacks a dented lantern from a crate. A much finer one catches his sleeve with its ornamental finial.
[Elian Vale | neutral] Elian Vale. Firecraft and introductory charms. This handsome object has spent the summer trying to undress me. We'll use the plain one.
[N] He lays it on the stone mat.
[Elian Vale | neutral] Three things today: start a small light, hold it, stop it. You'll use the practice wick in your own lantern. Its ordinary flame is separate from the enduring welcome-light some of you have seen in the hall.
[N] He points to a small mark stamped beneath the handle.
[Elian Vale | neutral] Thumb here to end the charm. If anything burns outside the glass, hands away and call me. The sand is here because paper doesn't always wait for a teacher.
[N] Bags are moved. Sleeves are rolled. Rowan checks that neither elbow will catch the other's lantern.
[N] Elian traces a short arc over the wick. Light appears, holds for two breaths, and disappears beneath his thumb.
[N] His hand has already moved on by the time you can place the moment when the flame began.
[IF arrival:night-intention = ask]
[N] In the margin of your notebook is the question mark you drew on your first night. You lift your hand and ask him to show the beginning again.
[Elian Vale | neutral] Before the gesture? Yes. That's the part I skipped.
[END IF]
[IF arrival:night-intention = stay]
[N] You start the motion, find yourself copying only its shape, and stop. The promise to try again allows time to ask for help first.
[N] Elian sees your raised hand and repeats the beginning.
[Elian Vale | neutral] Watch what I do before my fingers move. I choose how much warmth I want. I ought to have said that.
[END IF]
[IF arrival:night-intention = (none)]
[N] Several hands rise, yours among them. Elian looks down at his lantern and begins again, slowly enough to explain the part he has made look effortless.
[END IF]
[N] He describes warmth small enough to hold comfortably in a cup. His hand follows the arc; the wick catches.
[Elian Vale | neutral] Kindle. The motion helps you keep the intention steady. The paper tells you whether you've supplied too much heat.
[N] He holds up the unmarked square, then sets it down for the first student to examine.

--- SECTION partners | place: classroom | time: morning | date: 2026-09-04 ---
[IF arrival:common-room-rule = quiet]

[Rowan | warm] Shall we have one attempt without commentary? I can save my excellent advice until I've watched something happen.
[N] He lays his pencil across the card as a reminder.
[END IF]
[IF arrival:common-room-rule = welcome]
[N] You turn the instruction card towards Rowan. Making the study invitation clearer has left you with a useful suspicion of unexplained words.
[N] The observer's job is written on the back: watch the paper, count the breaths, call a stop if the heat escapes.
[Rowan | amused] I had planned to look encouraging. I'll watch the paper as well.
[END IF]
[N] Rowan takes the first turn. His flame rises bright and narrow, lights the underside of his chin, and goes out.
[N] One corner of the paper curls towards the glass.
[Rowan | amused] A brief but distinguished career.
[N] He leaves his hand above the wick. At the next desk, someone succeeds, and a delighted laugh travels across the room.
[N] Rowan tries to flatten the paper with one finger. It curls back up.
[Rowan | neutral] You should put it down. The corner, I mean. On the sheet.
[N] He pushes the pencil towards you.
[DECISION first-kindle:partner-response] What will help you work together?
[OPTION observe] Show him when the paper curled, then try less heat. | hint: Give him a change he can test. | journal: I showed Rowan where his first flame overheated the paper. His smaller second attempt held steady.
[OPTION swap] Take the next turn and let him watch. | hint: Share the awkward part of learning. | journal: I took the next turn at Kindle. Rowan noticed what improved, and we traded places until both of us could hold a small flame.

[IF first-kindle:partner-response = observe]
[N] You place a fresh square beside the curled one and point to where its edge moved first.
[N] Rowan draws a shorter arc in the air without casting.
[Rowan | shy] I know it isn't a competition. I keep forgetting that while other people are looking.
[N] This time his flame hardly rises above the wick. You count five breaths. The paper stays flat.
[N] When he stops the spell, he looks first at the square and then at you.
[Rowan | warm] Smaller. All right. I can do smaller again.
[END IF]
[IF first-kindle:partner-response = swap]
[N] You move your lantern onto the mat. Rowan straightens the paper and picks up the pencil.
[N] Your first gesture produces nothing. The second warms the handle without lighting the wick.
[Rowan | neutral] It started that time. Your hand slowed down here.
[N] He marks the point on the drawing. On the third attempt a small flame takes hold, and he is too occupied counting to watch the other desks.
[N] You exchange places again. His next flame is lower and lasts until he stops it.
[Rowan | warm] Keep the first attempts on the sheet. Otherwise it looks as if we knew what we were doing all along.
[END IF]
[N] Elian reaches your desk while Rowan is comparing the two squares of paper.
[IF arrival:first-observation = frame]
[Rowan | neutral] We checked the hall lantern like this. The frame, the seal, the things we could actually see.

[END IF]
[IF arrival:first-observation = record]

[Rowan | warm] She'll pretend she's annoyed about people copying her notes. Tell her you put the name in. That'll help.
[END IF]
[IF arrival:first-observation = (none)]
[Elian Vale | neutral] Put what happened beside what you changed. You'll want both when you try again tomorrow.
[END IF]
[N] He watches you cast. Your shoulders rise; the flame leans; he waits until you notice the tension and lower them.
[N] The wick steadies.
[N] He has you end the spell, move the lantern to the other side of the mat, and begin again.
[Elian Vale | neutral] There. You can carry that skill out of this chair now. Open practice before you try it alone, please.
[N] The small mark he makes beside your name is almost lost among the other marks on his class sheet.
[N] You have learned something that would have been impossible to explain at your kitchen table three weeks ago.

--- SECTION voice-in-flame | place: classroom | time: morning | date: 2026-09-04 ---
[N] There is time for one more attempt while Elian gathers the used paper.
[N] You kindle the wick. Rowan reaches for the pencil.
[N] Beneath the dry crackle of the flame, a voice says five words.
[N] Remember the way home.
[N] Rowan's hand stops above the desk.
[N] The flame remains small and yellow. At the next table, two students are laughing over a scorch mark shaped roughly like a rabbit.
[N] You press the stop mark.
[Rowan | tense] You heard it too?
[N] You nod. Elian puts down the paper he is carrying.
[Elian Vale | neutral] Leave the lantern where it is. Each of you, write what you heard before you tell the other.
[N] He gives Rowan a clean slip. You use the unmarked page beneath your practice notes.
[N] The words take very little space. Below them is a question Elian asks quietly enough that the next desk cannot hear.
[Elian Vale | neutral] Is there anything else you want in your account?
[DECISION first-kindle:voice-account] What will you add?
[OPTION uncertainty] The words were clear; their source wasn't. | hint: Keep the uncertainty in the report. | journal: I recorded the words Remember the way home and left their source unidentified.
[OPTION feeling] It felt as though the words were meant for me. | hint: Record an impression without calling it proof. | journal: I told Elian that the words felt directed at me, while keeping that impression separate from what I could establish.
[END DECISION]
[IF first-kindle:voice-account = uncertainty]
[N] Elian writes source unconfirmed beneath your account.
[N] Rowan turns his slip over. The same five words are there, with a question mark after home.
[Rowan | grave] I looked at the next table. Neither of them had turned round.
[Elian Vale | neutral] I'll include that. We can leave the question mark.
[END IF]
[IF first-kindle:voice-account = feeling]
[N] Elian records the impression below the words.
[Rowan | grave] It felt farther away to me. As if someone were calling through a door.
[Elian Vale | neutral] I'll keep both descriptions. You don't have to agree about that part.
[N] He places the slips side by side without crossing anything out.
[END IF]
[N] With your permission, he opens the lantern. The wick is ordinary prepared fibre; the maker's mark on the handle matches those on the other desks.
[N] Twice he repeats Kindle. Twice a quiet light burns in the glass.
[N] Neither attempt produces a voice.
[Elian Vale | neutral] Bring this to supervised practice for now. I'll ask the warding staff about old echoes in fittings.
[Rowan | grave] The light over our supper table went cold on the first night. Grey couldn't mend it.
[Elian Vale | neutral] Give me the report number. We'll compare the accounts before we decide they're the same problem.
[N] You unfold Grey's receipt. The crease is already soft from the number of times you have checked it.
[N] Elian copies the number beside today's date.
[N] The bell rings, releasing a scrape of chairs around you. He leaves your lantern on the mat until you are ready to take it.
