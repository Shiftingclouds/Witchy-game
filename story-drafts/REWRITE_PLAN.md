# Lanternmere manuscript rewrite

This pass follows the owner's uploaded `CHATGPT_STORY_REWRITE.md` and the linked `docs/design/CHATGPT_PROLOGUE.md`. Deliver one scene per numbered manuscript file, including the complete revised arrival. Runtime import is a separate handoff; these are actual prose drafts, not the game's generated `story/` exports.

## Questions governing each scene

- What does each person want before the player arrives? What changes because they meet?
- What can the player reasonably know, and what remains a suspicion? Who witnessed or was told a private fact?
- What does this choice change immediately, and where does its consequence return? Would the other option also be worth playing?
- What concrete obstacle gives the conversation a reason to happen? Can an action replace the narrator's explanation of its moral?
- What does the character avoid saying? How do their vocabulary, attention, mistakes and offers distinguish them?
- Does a returning reader recognise progress in the relationship without compulsory attraction or a prescribed player backstory?
- What earned detail from the beginning makes the ending matter? What will still require work after the magic succeeds?

## Limits

The owner's additional direction makes the house choice a continuing social route. Owlcombe pairs the player with Imogen and Idris; Rookhallow with Rowan and Cas; Heronmere with Noor and Petra; Larkspire with Saoirse and Toby. These pairs have recurring scenes across the year. Every student remains present in the shared world, and the central investigation remains accessible from every house. House membership does not confer knowledge of private conversations.

No new culprit, chosen-one destiny, resurrection, sacrifice, compulsory romance, named player family, or premature ritual revelation. House and familiar variations rejoin promptly. The seven original keepers are not automatically identical to the three workers found in later ledgers. The 250,000-word whole-game ambition remains a separate production goal; this rewrite must not inflate or misreport progress toward it.
